Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

1. Who we are

MYSI Factory is a personal tool operated by MYSI. Contact: mysi.digitaltwin@gmail.com

2. What we access

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives only the data covered by the permissions (scopes) the owner approves on TikTok's consent screen, such as basic profile information and the account's own video statistics.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to show the owner their own account information and analytics inside the app. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

4. Where data is stored

Data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by us.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner disconnects the account or deletes the app's local data. Disconnecting revokes the access token. You can also revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. To ask for anything else to be deleted, email mysi.digitaltwin@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
